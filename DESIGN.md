# CS-KMITL Room Booking (`DESIGN.md`)

Inherits the root [CS-KMITL Design System](../DESIGN.md).

## Direction

- **Product**: Lab & meeting room reservation, live room dashboard, QR check-in, and admin scheduling for CS-KMITL.
- **Character**: `structured, punctual, quiet` — clear time-slot availability and zero-friction booking flow.
- **Moments**: The room availability calendar/grid and the live room status dashboard.
- **Signature**: Hairline 1px time-slot cells, `tabular-nums` time ranges and room capacities, and semantic occupancy dots.
